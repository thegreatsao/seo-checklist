### ROW_LIMIT: the KW-070/GO-139 defect it waited on

The brand's most-searched query and the page that owns it are picked from the first 5000 rows; a `truthy` rule is
not withheld over `truncated`, so the cap could move KW-070 and GO-139 either way, and an unsearched brand (N/A)
could be one whose rows are past the cap. Fix, kept to the lines that read the row cap: over a truncated read the
script drops `searched`, `ranks_first` and `homepage_ranks_first` from `branded` and writes `branded.reason`,
which the runner already prints with the `NO_DATA` (`_absent`). No other field moves; MS-023 and KW-071 were
already withheld. Small and safe by the starting session's test: only the lines after `find_branded` change, and
the only verdicts that move are KW-070 and GO-139 on a property whose window fills the page.

Expected, declared before running: a full page (brand row + filler to 5000) — KW-070 and GO-139 `PASS` before,
`NO_DATA` after, `branded.reason` naming 5000; the brand row alone — `PASS` both. A full page with no brand row —
`N/A` before, `NO_DATA` after; one generic row — `N/A` both.

## Review

Codex `gpt-6.1-sol`, reasoning effort high (header checked), read-only, over `b56d6ec`. No P1. It traced the eight
`presentation` numbers to every field they move (verdicts and `score()` unchanged under each substitution), found
the census red for each script when a rule is made to read the field, confirmed the branded fix moves only KW-070
and GO-139 and only when `truncated`, the API's 25,000-row limit, and the ledgers. Findings, each reproduced before
the change:

1. **P2, KW-071's count off a full page is not a floor.** Four queries on two pages at positions 4.0 and 4.2 each,
   filler to 5000, and a third page per query at position 20 past the cap: KW-071 FAILed four contests the whole
   window does not have (`contested_queries` 4 against 0). A contest is a spread of at most three positions, and
   a page past the cap widens it. The script now withholds `contested_queries` over `truncated`, with a
   `truncated_reason`: `test_a_contest_count_off_a_full_page_is_withheld` (red with the count kept; its
   whole-window control passes). MS-023's count is a floor — a row past the cap only adds a page to a split, and
   the brand spreads are sorted against is supplied, published, or the top-click row the API returns first — so
   its FAIL stands: `test_a_split_count_off_a_full_page_is_a_floor_and_stands`. ROW_LIMIT's line now says which.
   KW-071 moves from FAIL or WARN to NO_DATA on a property whose window fills the page.
2. **P3, `branded.reason` claimed rows past the cap at exactly 5000**, where the window may hold no more. It
   says "may hold more" now. (`>=` stays: without a second request, 5000 and more look alike.) Also noted: the
   contract's "no other field moves" was too strong — dropping `searched` also suppresses the two branded
   `issues` entries, which no item reads.
