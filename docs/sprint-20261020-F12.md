## Scope

F09 relabelled `DEFAULT_MAX_PAGES` and `DEFAULT_DEPTH` `convention` on the argument that every crawl-fed verdict
over a capped crawl is withheld, unmoved, or a floor. The second review reproduced two that are none of these:

1. **BL-081** (`summary.overused_exact_match_targets eq 0`, warn `lte 10`, `applies_when links_analyzed gt 0`).
   Overuse is a share — one anchor on at least 80% of a target's editorial links — and which links are editorial is a
   share across pages. Reading more pages can lower either, so the count over a capped crawl is not a floor: the
   reviewer measured 11 overused targets on 100 of 110 pages, 0 on all 110. And the pages read carrying no link made
   the item N/A for a site whose other pages carry them. Fix: over a truncated crawl `anchor_text_audit.py` withholds
   the count (with `truncated_reason`), and `links_analyzed` when it is 0.
2. **CI-018**'s `sitemap_urls_never_crawled` (medium) = sitemap URLs − log paths − the robots-refused URLs the crawl
   discovered. A capped crawl that never reached a disallowed sitemap URL counts it never crawled, so CI-018 WARNed
   on a crawler obeying `Disallow`. Fix: over a truncated crawl the count is taken over the sitemap URLs the crawl
   fetched (robots allows those), so what it finds is real.

Recorded as accepted by this tree's rules, not changed here (both written into the `DEFAULT_MAX_PAGES` basis line):
a WARN counted over the pages read is a floor and can understate a FAIL (VRD-14; CI-018, CN-039, AR-149, TE-168),
and with the opt-in `--sample` the cap bounds the pool a sample is drawn from, so it decides which pages a sampled
verdict is about. The owner may rule otherwise; then the two caps go back to `inherited`.

Also from the review: the near-duplicate guard test now reads `warn` rules too.
