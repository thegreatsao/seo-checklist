## Scope

- `audit_item_readings.digest(item, entry)`: a short sha256 over the item's title, check and `measures` (registry) and
  the reading's verdict, reading and shapes — canonical JSON, so key order and whitespace do not move it. The
  `second_reading` key itself is not part of it.
- `problems()` names, per entry carrying `second_reading`:
  - a record that is not an object, or carries a key outside `reader`, `session`, `read`, `verdict`, `dispute`;
  - an empty or missing `reader`, `session` or `read`;
  - a verdict outside `answers`, `measures`, `owed`;
  - a `read` that is not the current digest: "the reading changed under its second reading".
- A missing second reading and a disputed one (verdict differs, or a non-empty `dispute`) are counted, not problems.
- The summary prints `second readings: N of 145 current — A agree, D disputed`, and `--digest ID` prints one item's
  digest (exit 1 on an id with no reading). Nothing writes the file.
- `ESTABLISHES` / `DOES_NOT_ESTABLISH` and the module docstring: a recorded second reading is of the current reading;
  not that its reader was independent (the Codex session id is the trace) nor that either reading is true.
- REG-6's Reader line in `openspec/specs/registry/spec.md`; `docs/quality-document.md` for the tool.
