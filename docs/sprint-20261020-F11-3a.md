## Scope

Twelve `inherited` numbers that do decide a verdict, each traced to the item it moves, checked for a published
standard, and asked whether a corpus this machine can reach would settle it. None has a standard: Google
publishes no word count, no URL length that costs anything and no `llms.txt` metric, and the `llms.txt` proposal
names none. For each, what a corpus would show is a distribution of what sites publish, and the claim each number
makes is a different one — *too thin for its purpose*, *too long to read*, *useful to a model* — which no
distribution of practice answers. So each goes to `convention` with that argument written, not to `measured`.

## Review

Codex `gpt-6.1-sol`, reasoning effort high (header checked), read-only, over `c550609`. It agreed with eleven of
the twelve, checked the arithmetic (GEO-002 bonuses each flip 60/PASS to 55/FAIL; CN-039 0 PASS, 1–5 WARN, ≥6
FAIL; CI-012 two flags 76 PASS, three 64 FAIL) and the ledgers. Findings, each reproduced before the change:

1. **P2, `MIN_PARAGRAPH_WORDS` is a classifier a labelled corpus scores.** It cited the Zyte (Scrapinghub)
   article-extraction benchmark, HTML with hand-written article bodies. Measured
   (`tools/calibrate_paragraph_words.py`, `tools/calibration/paragraph-words.json`, benchmark commit
   `4a3bc979`): over 181 pages, paragraph-level F1 is best at cutoff 0 (0.861; 0.810 at 8), where every
   non-empty paragraph is body; at 8 the filter dropped 444 of 2,588 labelled body paragraphs (17%) for 3.5
   points of precision. The rule — best F1, ties to the smaller cutoff — is written in the tool before the run.
   Checked against accidental matches: a sample of short "body" paragraphs is mostly real copy (credits, steps,
   a whole Japanese sentence, which `split()` counts as one word and the old cutoff dropped) with some false
   ones ("National", "90"). (First written as "with a quarter counted false, 0 still wins"; the second review
   found 3 wins then, F1 0.8426 against 0.8399, both far above 8's 0.8270. The report now stores that
   sensitivity and `--check` re-derives it; the line says the corpus supports 0 to 3 and rules out 8.) **Now `measured`, 0**: KW-076
   moves from FAIL to PASS where the keyword's only mention is a paragraph of eight words or fewer.
   `test_a_keyword_in_a_short_paragraph_is_in_the_body` (red at 8).
2. **P2, `reads()` narrowed through `field` for every operator.** The runner applies `field` only for
   `none_matching` and `value_map`; `{"path": "subparts", "field": "ttfb_ms", "matches": ...}` reads the whole
   `subparts` and the census missed it. Fixed; that mutation of TECH-003 is red now.
3. **P3, stale prose:** `test_inherited_basis.py` said 77 numbers carry `inherited`; `known-issues.json` said
   twenty uncounted constants (nineteen); THIN_CONTENT_WORDS' "on every site" is "on any site with pages near
   it". Corrected.
