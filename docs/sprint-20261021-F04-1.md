### HTTP-10 — the decompression limit is held only as a memory ceiling

What is not held: `parse_sitemap_xml` reads `SITEMAP_MAX_UNCOMPRESSED + 1` bytes out of a gzip stream, and the
two tests require the document invalid and the peak under 150 MB. A read of 60,000,001 (the review of 0.172.0)
stays green.

- **H10-a.** A gzipped urlset that expands to exactly 52,428,800 bytes parses as a `urlset` with its URL; one
  that expands to 52,428,801 is `invalid` naming the limit. Expected **green on `efce43e`** (the comparison is
  right); red under `>` → `>=` and under the constant set to 60,000,000.
- **H10-b.** Over a 200 MB expansion, the bytes the gzip reader hands back in total are at most 52,428,801 —
  counted by wrapping the reader, so the number tested is the limit's own, not memory. Expected **green on
  `efce43e`**; red under `read(60_000_001)`.
- Holds in full → `enforced`. No verdict moves.

## H13-c, decided after the review (declared before its tests were written)

Anton's decision, relayed 2026-10-21: **"C: повтор, потом удержать"**. A crawled page that gives no answer —
the fetch layer's `timeout` or `refused` (connection refused or reset), no HTTP status — is requested once more.
If the retry also gives no answer, the crawl is `truncated`, its summary names the unread pages, and the
inventory readers that pass by absence withhold PASS as for a page the guard refused; a FAIL or WARN found in the
pages read stands. A guard refusal (`blocked`), an HTTP status of any kind, a TLS failure, and a `robots` decline
are not retried. The retry goes through the crawl's own request counter like any request.

- **C-a.** A page that times out once and answers on the retry: the crawl is not `truncated`, the page is read.
  Expected **red on `f831882`** (no retry: the page is a broken row with no content).
- **C-b.** A page that gives no answer twice: `truncated`, a new `summary.unanswered_pages` count names it, and
  MS-022 over that inventory is `NO_DATA` with the reason in its evidence. Expected **red** (today: complete, PASS).
- **C-c.** A page refused by the guard, a page answering 503, and a robots-declined page are each requested once.
  Expected **green** for the refusal and the 503 once the retry exists only for the no-answer kinds; red if it
  retried them.
- Verdicts that move on a live audit: the 14 inventory readers that pass by absence — CI-008, CI-015, CI-018,
  MS-022, MS-026, MS-029, CN-039, CN-041, BL-081, AR-149, AR-162, TE-168, TE-176, MD-187 (derived from the registry
  with the runner's `passes_by_absence`) — PASS → NO_DATA when a crawled page gave no answer twice. A page that gave
  no answer once and then answered is now read, so any of them can also move either way on what that page holds.

### H13-c outcome
