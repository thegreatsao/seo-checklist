## Scope

1. **The crawl inventory's producer** (brief, step 1). `PRODUCERS` takes a second kind of producer: a script in
   `scripts/`, printed whole after the reading script under `### Input of <script>: written by this code,
   `site_crawl.py``, and covered by the digest. Whole, not `crawl()` alone: what lands in the inventory is decided
   across `_read_page`, `_finish`, `page_key` and `has_noindex`, and a function cut out of its module hides them.
   The eleven scripts map to `site_crawl.py`. A guard test from the registry: every script whose `check.args` names
   `{inventory_json}` has it. A missing producer script is an error.
2. **How an assertion is evaluated** (brief, step 2). The brief's blind part points the reader at `evaluate()` and
   `normal_severity()` in `checklist_runner.py` (in the working directory) and states the one translation that
   misled batch 3: `none_severity` reads each issue's severity through `SEVERITY_ALIAS` — `error` is `high`,
   `warning`/`warn` `medium`, `info`/`notice` `low`; an absent path is no verdict. A test holds that the brief names
   the alias and that each mapping it states is the code's. Then AR-146 and CN-048 are re-read, blind then open, in
   one fresh Codex session, and their records replaced.

   from Chrome's trace insights (`SKILL.md` "Core Web Vitals from a local trace"): the section holds no

   trace and the three keys, and `cwv_metrics.py` is not in `PRODUCERS`. Its three items are not in this batch.
